# DUYO Beta — 5 Oila uchun qo'llanma

DUYO — bolalar uchun AI hamroh. Bosqich B beta versiya, 5 oila bilan birga sinab ko'ramiz.

## DUYO nima qiladi

7-16 yosh bolalar uchun AI suhbatdosh. Ikki rejimi bor:

- **Yozma chat** — yoziladigan suhbat
- **Ovozli chat** — gapirish (mikrofonga ruxsat kerak)

Bola gapirayotgani xavfli signal bilan kelganda DUYO yumshoq tarzda yordam taklif qiladi, jiddiy holatda **ota-onaga SMS** yuboradi.

## Birinchi marta ochish

### iOS (iPhone / iPad)

1. **TestFlight** ilovasini App Store'dan o'rnating.
2. Sizga yuborilgan **TestFlight taklifnomasi** linkini bosing.
3. TestFlight ichida "Install" tugmasini bosing.
4. DUYO iconini bosib oching.

### Android

1. Sizga yuborilgan **APK** faylini telefonga yuklang (Telegram yoki Google Drive orqali).
2. "Install from unknown sources" ruxsatini bering.
3. APK'ni oching va o'rnating.
